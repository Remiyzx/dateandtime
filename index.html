📅 Date (Tarikh)

What is today's date?
Hari ini berapa haribulan?[cite: 1]
[Today] Jīntiān
[which month] jǐ yuè
[which day of month?] jǐ hào?
Friday, 6th November, 2026[cite: 1]
English Order: Day of Week ➔ Day of Month ➔ Month ➔ Year (Smallest to Largest)[cite: 1]
[Year 2026] èr líng èr liù nián
[Month 11] shíyī yuè
[Day 6] liù hào,
[Day of Week 5] xīngqī wǔ
Chinese Order: Year ➔ Month ➔ Day of Month ➔ Day of Week (Largest to Smallest!)[cite: 1]
Tahun ➔ Bulan ➔ Haribulan ➔ Hari (Paling besar ke paling kecil!)[cite: 1]
💡 Crucial Word Order Rule: [ Number + Unit ] vs [ Day of Week ][cite: 1]
✅ Standard Rule: Number FIRST, Unit AFTER
11 + yuè ➔ shíyī yuè
6 + hào ➔ liù hào
Nombor dahulu, kemudian unit masa.[cite: 1]
⚠️ Exception: Day of Week (Unit FIRST!)[cite: 1]
xīngqī + wǔ (5) ➔ xīngqī wǔ
Special: Sunday = xīngqītiān[cite: 1]
Khusus hari: Sebut 'xīngqī' dahulu, baru nombor 1-6.[cite: 1]

💡 Quick Guide: Key Difference
jǐ diǎn
👉 Asks specifically for CLOCK TIME (Numbers)![cite: 1]
Khusus tanya pukul berapa (nombor jam: 7:00, 10:30)[cite: 1]
shénme shíhou
👉 General question for "WHEN" (Day, date, or period)![cite: 1]
Tanya bila secara umum (hari, tarikh, waktu)[cite: 1]
